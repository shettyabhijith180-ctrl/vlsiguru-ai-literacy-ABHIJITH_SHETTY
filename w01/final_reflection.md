## 1. What Is the Most Useful Thing I Learned This Week?

The most useful thing I learned was how to distinguish traditional rule-based software, machine-learning-based AI, and generative AI. I also learned the basic process of how a Large Language Model generates a response and how AI can support VLSI Physical Design tasks. ## 2. What Did I Initially Misunderstand?

Initially, I misunderstood the relationship between Deep Learning and Generative AI. I thought Generative AI was simply the next level after Deep Learning. I learned that Deep Learning is an approach used to build AI models, whereas Generative AI refers to AI systems that generate new content. Many modern Generative AI systems use deep-learning models.

## 3. What Did AI Help Me Do?

AI helped me understand technical concepts, organise my mission answers, and connect AI concepts to my VLSI Physical Design background. It also helped me write and understand a Python program that calculates setup slack and identifies a simplified setup timing violation based on the calculated slack.

## 4. Where Did I Need to Verify or Correct AI?

In Mission 4, I checked important claims about how Large Language Models process tokens and generate text against technical documentation. In Mission 5, I verified the explanation of setup time using official Intel and AMD documentation. These activities taught me that AI-generated explanations should be checked against reliable sources before being accepted or applied in engineering work.
